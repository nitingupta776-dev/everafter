# Fushimi Inari fridge magnet CAD brief

Source references:
- `reference_images/IMG_2467.heic.png` front view
- `reference_images/IMG_2468.heic.png` and `IMG_2469.heic.png` side views
- `reference_images/IMG_2470.heic.png` top view
- `reference_images/IMG_2471.heic.png` back view
- `reference_images/IMG_2472.heic.png` lower/front depth view

Assumptions from handheld photos:
- Overall souvenir magnet is modeled at about 72 mm wide by 72 mm tall.
- Back-to-front torii body depth is about 16 mm, with the fox charm projecting to about 24 mm total depth.
- Back magnet pad is modeled as a 45 mm diameter dark disk, based on the back photo.
- The gold plaque reads `伏見稲荷` and is modeled as raised black text on a gold plate.
- This is a visual GLB/CAD reconstruction from photos, not a metrology-grade replica for manufacturing.
